Why track DBU usage?

DBUs provide a usage measure to review as you develop and operate an agent. Check the reporting period before comparing usage across development sessions. A change in usage should be interpreted alongside the work the agent performed.

DBU usage and monetary cost

A usage quantity is not a final invoice amount. Converting it to money depends on the applicable billing terms and rates. FinOpsWay does not reflect private contract discounts. Review your Databricks billing information when you need to reconcile charges.

Get started in your workspace

Install FinOpsWay for Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Follow the setup guide to configure your workspace connection and access. Then open the relevant Databricks page to see the cost context.

Common questions

Is the usage shown in real time?

Cost and usage views depend on the data available to the extension. Recent billing can be incomplete. Check data freshness before interpreting the latest period as a complete total.

How should I interpret the displayed number?

Recent billing can arrive with a delay of several hours. Check the period and freshness indicator. Displayed costs are not an invoice and do not include private discounts. Read the cost methodology for calculation and attribution limits.
