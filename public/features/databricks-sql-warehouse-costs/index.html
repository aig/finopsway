Planning cost versus reported usage

A configuration-time cost view helps you consider the resource you are about to use. A final monthly cost also depends on its usage over time. Treat setup information as planning context and reconcile actual spend with billing once usage has been reported.

What to check during warehouse setup

Review the compute configuration alongside the workload it will serve. Record your expected usage period so that later comparisons have a useful baseline. After running the workload, check the billing coverage before comparing reported cost with your expectation.

Get started in your workspace

Install FinOpsWay for Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Follow the setup guide to configure your workspace connection and access. Then open the relevant Databricks page to see the cost context.

Common questions

Is a warehouse total the cost of one SQL query?

No. A resource total is not automatically a per-query allocation. When several queries or users share compute, assigning its cost requires an allocation policy.

How should I interpret the displayed number?

Recent billing can arrive with a delay of several hours. Check the period and freshness indicator. Displayed costs are not an invoice and do not include private discounts. Read the cost methodology for calculation and attribution limits.
