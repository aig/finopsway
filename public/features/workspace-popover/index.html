FinOpsWay features

Workspace panel

See cost context where you already work in Databricks.

What the workspace panel shows

The workspace panel brings FinOpsWay cost context into the Databricks interface. It gives you a compact view of the workspace cost, the reporting period and the freshness of the latest billing data.

How the panel works

  1. Connect FinOpsWay to your Databricks workspace.
  2. Open a Databricks page where the FinOpsWay extension can add cost context.
  3. Select a reporting period. Review the chart, cost totals and resource details.
  4. Check the reported time. A cost can still be delayed while Databricks completes billing.

Reporting periods

The panel lets you compare the latest available data across several windows. The example view includes 24 hours, 7 days and 14 days. A custom period can be used when you need a broader comparison.

Billing freshness

Databricks reports billing data after a delay. The panel shows the latest available period and when the data was last refreshed. Do not use an incomplete period as a final budget decision.

Reviewing the details

The panel can show cost by SKU, recent chart data and the end of the selected reporting window. Use the details to compare resources, investigate a larger spend or choose a different workload configuration.

What the panel does not show

The panel is a cost view, not a billing invoice. It can use estimates for cloud compute and may not include every charge, contract adjustment or separately billed cloud resource.

Add FinOpsWay to your browser

Install the free extension from your browser’s store. No FinOpsWay account is required.
