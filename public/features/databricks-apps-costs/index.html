Databricks Apps Costs

App cost and the full application

An application may also use a database, a job or SQL compute. Review those resources separately when assessing the cost of the service you deliver. Do not assume a cost shown on the Apps page includes every dependency used by the application.

How to review an application change

Compare similar periods before and after the change, once billing coverage is complete. Consider whether usage of the app changed too. If the application uses Lakebase, review its database and branches alongside the app view to understand the resources involved.

Get started in your workspace

Install FinOpsWay for Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Follow the setup guide to configure your workspace connection and access. Then open the relevant Databricks page to see the cost context.

Common questions

Does FinOpsWay collect my application data?

FinOpsWay has no backend that receives your workspace data. The extension works with your configured Databricks access. Read the security page for the data flow and permission details.

How should I interpret the displayed number?

Recent billing can arrive with a delay of several hours. Check the period and freshness indicator. Displayed costs are not an invoice and do not include private discounts. Read the cost methodology for calculation and attribution limits.
