Which workload should you investigate?

Use the cost context beside your jobs and pipelines to decide where to look next. Compare similar reporting periods. A daily ingestion pipeline and an occasional backfill have different schedules, so their totals need workload context.

From a workload total to a run

When a job total needs investigation, open its runs and review the execution you are interested in. Keep the job-level view for the broader workload and the run-level view for an individual execution. Do not assume one recent run explains the whole reporting period.

Get started in your workspace

Install FinOpsWay for Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Follow the setup guide to configure your workspace connection and access. Then open the relevant Databricks page to see the cost context.

Common questions

Why can a job cost differ from its cluster cost?

A cluster may also run other work. Job attribution depends on the usage records available for the workload. Shared compute totals cannot automatically be assigned in full to a single job.

How should I interpret the displayed number?

Recent billing can arrive with a delay of several hours. Check the period and freshness indicator. Displayed costs are not an invoice and do not include private discounts. Read the cost methodology for calculation and attribution limits.
